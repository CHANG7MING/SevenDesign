# Archetype: Landing Hero

- Purpose: establish product identity, value, and next action within the first viewport
- Audience: first-time visitors, evaluators, prospective customers
- Density: low to medium
- Flavor mix: one primary flavor plus one support flavor
- Best themes: `AI_NATIVE`, `DEVTOOL_PRO`, `CREATIVE_MOTION`, `LUXE_PERFORMANCE`
- Avoid: equal emphasis across all content blocks

## Layout

- Header: slim nav with one dominant CTA
- Main grid: two-column or centered single-column with strong visual anchor
- Side content: proof row, trust badges, micro metrics, or compact secondary CTA
- Footer / close: transition into proof, feature, or product demo section

## Dominant Components

- Eyebrow or category tag
- Display headline
- Support copy
- Primary and secondary CTA
- Product scene, demo shell, or media focal block

## Motion

- Entrance style: staged reveal from headline to proof to visual
- Hover style: CTA clarity and focal object response only
- Scroll behavior: gentle handoff into the next section, not dramatic parallax stacking

## Content Rhythm

- Opening: category + promise
- Middle: product proof or visual
- Closing: action and trust confirmation
