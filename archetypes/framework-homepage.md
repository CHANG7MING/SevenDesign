# Archetype: Framework Homepage

- Purpose: position a design framework, workflow product, or agent skill system as a real product with clear adoption value
- Audience: developers, designers, AI power users, teams evaluating a workflow layer
- Density: medium
- Flavor mix: `Developer Dark` + `Editorial Minimal` + `Cinematic Future`
- Best themes: `PRODUCTIZED_FRAMEWORK`, `DEVTOOL_PRO`, `AI_NATIVE`
- Avoid: repository-style information dumps in the first screen

## Layout

- Header: compact product nav with focused CTA
- Main grid: strong hero plus productized visual proof
- Side content: install, usage proof, adoption signals, skill cards, or example strip
- Footer / close: FAQ, install, support, or examples

## Dominant Components

- large hero headline
- utility subtitle
- CTA cluster
- proof row or trust strip
- skill / module cards
- examples or showcase section

## Motion

- Entrance style: staged but controlled
- Hover style: card emphasis and CTA response
- Scroll behavior: stronger section rhythm than standard product marketing

## Content Rhythm

- Opening: positioning and promise
- Middle: proof, skills, examples, why it matters
- Closing: install, FAQ, and action
