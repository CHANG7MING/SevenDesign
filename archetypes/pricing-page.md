# Archetype: Pricing Page

- Purpose: explain plans, reduce decision friction, and guide the buyer toward the right tier
- Audience: evaluators, buyers, teams, finance stakeholders
- Density: medium
- Flavor mix: `Editorial Minimal` + product-specific support flavor
- Best themes: `SAAS_WARM`, `FINTECH_TRUST`, `DEVTOOL_PRO`
- Avoid: too many spotlighted plans or marketing language that hides real differences

## Layout

- Header: concise framing and billing toggle
- Main grid: plan cards or pricing table with one recommended choice
- Side content: feature matrix, FAQ, trust band, procurement notes
- Footer / close: enterprise contact or final CTA

## Dominant Components

- pricing cards
- billing toggle
- comparison table
- FAQ accordion
- proof or trust strip

## Motion

- Entrance style: staggered reveal for cards and proof sections
- Hover style: modest emphasis on the active plan
- Scroll behavior: optional sticky pricing toggle or anchor nav

## Content Rhythm

- Opening: pricing promise and plan framing
- Middle: plan comparison and differentiation
- Closing: FAQ, proof, enterprise path
