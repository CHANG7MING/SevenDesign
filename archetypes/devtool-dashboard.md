# Archetype: Devtool Dashboard

- Purpose: monitor systems, manage workflows, inspect operational state
- Audience: developers, ops teams, product engineers, technical admins
- Density: medium to high
- Flavor mix: `Developer Dark` + `Data Dense Enterprise`
- Best themes: `DEVTOOL_PRO`, `FINTECH_TRUST`
- Avoid: homepage-style drama inside dense task surfaces

## Layout

- Header: compact global controls with workspace switch and search
- Main grid: 12-column metric-and-table system or split panels
- Side content: filters, logs, details, assistant, or entity inspector
- Footer / close: low emphasis, often omitted in-app

## Dominant Components

- Metrics cards
- Structured tables
- Filter bars
- Tabs
- Alerts or logs

## Motion

- Entrance style: minimal; priority on immediate usability
- Hover style: row, card, and control feedback only
- Scroll behavior: sticky controls and anchored contexts

## Content Rhythm

- Opening: top-line operational state
- Middle: tables, trends, and drilldowns
- Closing: diagnostics, logs, or contextual details
