# Brand Recipe: Tesla

- Category: automotive / premium technology brand
- Primary references: Tesla, Apple, SpaceX
- Core mood: monumental, futuristic, minimal, performance-driven
- Primary accent: monochrome, with rare `Hyper Red`
- Secondary accent: deep graphite
- Background direction: stark white or black stage
- Typography signal: large, quiet, high-confidence display hierarchy
- Surface style: sparse, sculpted, product-first
- Motion character: slow, cinematic, controlled
- UI density: low
- Best use cases: product launches, premium hardware, automotive, flagship homepages
- Avoid: crowded feature grids, decorative badges, busy dashboards in hero contexts

## Borrowed Signals

- Signal 1: product drama through restraint
- Signal 2: minimal text with maximum confidence
- Signal 3: large-scale staging and spatial calm

## Component Implications

- Buttons: minimal and authoritative
- Cards: large, quiet, materially clean
- Navigation: nearly invisible
- Data displays: used sparingly and simplified
- Marketing sections: product renders, specification bands, immersive transitions

## Hero Guidance

- Headline style: short and monumental
- Visual direction: one product object, one system, one bold frame
- CTA tone: confident and premium
- Motion note: cinematic reveal, no UI gimmickry

## Inner Page Guidance

- Dashboard/workspace: only adapt this style selectively
- Docs/content: clean and sparse
- Forms/settings: premium minimal, no fluff
