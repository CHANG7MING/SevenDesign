# Brand Recipe: Notion

- Category: productivity / knowledge workspace
- Primary references: Notion, Airbnb, Claude
- Core mood: approachable, quiet, warm, flexible
- Primary accent: `Pulse Coral` or monochrome
- Secondary accent: warm gray
- Background direction: off-white paper-like surfaces
- Typography signal: editorial readability with calm UI text
- Surface style: light, subtle, low-friction
- Motion character: soft and nearly invisible
- UI density: low to medium
- Best use cases: docs, wikis, collaboration, onboarding, knowledge products
- Avoid: aggressive glow, hard enterprise chrome, hyper-saturated accent stacks

## Borrowed Signals

- Signal 1: warmth through whitespace and tone
- Signal 2: productivity surfaces that feel flexible rather than rigid
- Signal 3: low visual friction for long reading sessions

## Component Implications

- Buttons: softer secondary hierarchy, clear but quiet primary
- Cards: paper-like, minimally elevated
- Navigation: simple, friendly, highly readable
- Data displays: light framing and low noise
- Marketing sections: approachable clarity over futuristic spectacle

## Hero Guidance

- Headline style: friendly and readable
- Visual direction: workspace collage, docs panel, collaboration flow
- CTA tone: welcoming and helpful
- Motion note: gentle fades and small stagger only

## Inner Page Guidance

- Dashboard/workspace: prioritize readability and editing comfort
- Docs/content: best-in-class long-form comfort
- Forms/settings: simple, direct, kind
