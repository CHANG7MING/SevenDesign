# Brand Recipe: RunwayML

- Category: AI creative tool / media generation
- Primary references: RunwayML, ElevenLabs, Framer, Spotify
- Core mood: cinematic, immersive, intelligent, expressive
- Primary accent: `Velocity Purple`
- Secondary accent: `Infra Blue` or soft magenta
- Background direction: dark atmospheric canvas with media-led lighting
- Typography signal: bold display type paired with clean interface text
- Surface style: immersive panels, rich hero compositions, controlled glow
- Motion character: visible, layered, media-forward
- UI density: low to medium
- Best use cases: AI generation, media tools, showcase-led product sites
- Avoid: dry enterprise framing, generic dashboard screenshots, flat monochrome marketing

## Borrowed Signals

- Signal 1: cinematic darkness with compositional confidence
- Signal 2: media as proof, not decoration
- Signal 3: motion as emotional framing

## Component Implications

- Buttons: clear and high-contrast against immersive backgrounds
- Cards: preview-led, edge-lit, often asymmetric
- Navigation: quiet enough to not compete with media
- Data displays: secondary concern; keep them lightweight
- Marketing sections: image/video blocks can dominate when hierarchy is clear

## Hero Guidance

- Headline style: bold and evocative with a sharp product promise
- Visual direction: large preview canvas, animated scene, output comparison, media reel
- CTA tone: creative empowerment
- Motion note: ambient motion plus one focal transition, never chaos

## Inner Page Guidance

- Dashboard/workspace: preserve cinematic shell while protecting workflow clarity
- Docs/content: reduce drama and increase readability
- Forms/settings: simplify; keep creative surfaces for high-value moments
