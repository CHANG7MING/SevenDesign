# Brand Recipe: Apple

- Category: Premium consumer technology
- Primary references: Apple, Airbnb, BMW
- Core mood: minimal, monumental, elegant, calm
- Primary accent: monochrome or `Infra Blue` used sparingly
- Secondary accent: subtle neutral warmth
- Background direction: white, graphite, or stage-lit black
- Typography signal: large, confident, almost silent hierarchy
- Surface style: material-like cleanliness, invisible precision
- Motion character: restrained, spatial, high-fidelity
- UI density: low
- Best use cases: launches, premium hardware, flagship pages, prestige product narratives
- Avoid: crowded screens, excessive chips and badges, aggressive gradient noise

## Borrowed Signals

- Signal 1: product-first framing with very high confidence
- Signal 2: quiet visual luxury through subtraction
- Signal 3: motion used to reveal form and depth

## Component Implications

- Buttons: minimal, tactile, confident
- Cards: large-radius or clean-edge blocks with strong spacing
- Navigation: almost disappears into the system
- Data displays: only when needed, and always simplified
- Marketing sections: big type, product render, generous whitespace

## Hero Guidance

- Headline style: short, iconic, unforgettable
- Visual direction: one object or one interface scene with monumental staging
- CTA tone: calm and premium
- Motion note: slow reveal, subtle parallax or lighting shift, no gimmicks

## Inner Page Guidance

- Dashboard/workspace: only use this influence selectively for premium shells
- Docs/content: simplify and soften the environment
- Forms/settings: invisible polish, high legibility, low ornament
