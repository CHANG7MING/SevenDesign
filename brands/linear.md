# Brand Recipe: Linear

- Category: productivity / product operations
- Primary references: Linear, Raycast, Apple
- Core mood: sharp, efficient, minimal, premium
- Primary accent: `Velocity Purple` or monochrome
- Secondary accent: muted cool gray
- Background direction: dark graphite or clean off-white
- Typography signal: tight, polished, highly controlled
- Surface style: reduced chrome, precise hierarchy, calm emphasis
- Motion character: fast, elegant, almost invisible
- UI density: medium
- Best use cases: issue tracking, productivity apps, command-heavy tools, changelogs
- Avoid: oversized marketing gestures, playful illustrations, soft vague surfaces

## Borrowed Signals

- Signal 1: premium precision through subtraction
- Signal 2: operations software that still feels desirably crafted
- Signal 3: motion used for confidence, not spectacle

## Component Implications

- Buttons: restrained, compact, clear hierarchy
- Cards: quiet containers with strong spacing
- Navigation: crisp active states, excellent scan speed
- Data displays: timeline and list rhythm over dashboard drama
- Marketing sections: product confidence, not narrative overload

## Hero Guidance

- Headline style: concise and exact
- Visual direction: timeline, command workflow, issue graph, compact product scene
- CTA tone: focused and understated
- Motion note: fast reveal, no soft bouncy transitions

## Inner Page Guidance

- Dashboard/workspace: tight interaction loops and keyboard-first affordances
- Docs/content: minimal and legible
- Forms/settings: compact and polished
